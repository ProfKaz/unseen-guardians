# Unseen Guardians

## The KazDemos Chronicles

**Everyday heroes. Invisible work. A company that evolves.**

A bilingual educational manga series about the people behind IT, security and data governance at **KazDemos**, a fictional company learning to protect what it is building.

A hundred people. Thirteen protagonists. Connected short stories with decisions, consequences and lessons that carry into the next chapter.

## Español

Unseen Guardians cuenta las aventuras de los equipos que hacen posible que KazDemos siga funcionando y crezca. Las historias combinan vida de oficina, metáforas de superhéroes y aprendizaje práctico.

Las versiones de lectura estarán disponibles en español e inglés. Los scripts, comentarios y documentación técnica se mantendrán en inglés.

## English

Unseen Guardians follows the teams whose daily work keeps KazDemos running and growing. The stories combine office life, superhero metaphors and practical learning.

Reading editions will be available in Spanish and English. Scripts, comments and technical documentation will be maintained in English.

## Status

The first chapter is in editorial preparation. This repository contains the public reading shell and Jekyll configuration. GitHub Pages has not been enabled yet.

Character foundations come from [ClaudIA](https://github.com/MH-Demos/ClaudIA). KazDemos has its own story timeline. All cases are fictional.
